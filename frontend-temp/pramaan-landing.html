Right now, verification means chasing the same document across ten portals.

Procurement officers on GeM are required to examine and cross-check bidder eligibility across Udyam registration, GST filings, PAN and income tax compliance, EPFO/ESIC status, Make in India content, Startup India and NSIC standing, OEM authorisation, and blacklisting records — for every bidder, on every tender.

None of these portals talk to each other. The work is manual, repetitive, and slow, and small inconsistencies are easy to miss until much later in the process — which is exactly when they're most expensive to catch.

How Pramaan verifies a bidder

The same checks a procurement officer runs today, retrieved and cross-referenced automatically, with a human still making the final call.

01

Bidder submits, as usual

Documents and registration numbers are submitted through GeM in the normal bidding flow — no separate process for the bidder to learn.

02

Pramaan retrieves and cross-checks

Udyam, GSTN, PAN, MCA21, EPFO, ESIC, DigiLocker and other relevant portals are queried directly, and the results are matched against what was submitted.

03

The AI verification engine reviews the record

Missing, inconsistent or non-compliant information is identified against both statutory requirements and the specific tender's eligibility conditions.

04

A compliance dashboard is generated

The officer sees a compliance score, a risk level, document-by-document verification status, and what's still pending — in one place.

05

The procurement officer decides

Pramaan recommends; it doesn't rule. Qualification or disqualification stays a human decision, backed by a complete, auditable record of every check performed.
